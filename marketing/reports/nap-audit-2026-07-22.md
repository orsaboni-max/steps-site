## 3. The real finding: STEPS has TWO physical branches, and the web doesn't clearly separate them

Confirmed via a netanyanet.co.il article ("הכירו את 'סטפס' - הסטודיו המנצח בנתניה"): STEPS operates **two locations** — Poleg Mall (the one `stepsnetanya.co.il` and its JSON-LD represent) and **Naimi Mall / Kiryat HaSharon, Tom Lantos Blvd 60** (co-owned/run by the same two partners).

This is not "duplicate = ranking poison" in the classic sense (it's a real second address, not a stale copy) — but it explains why generic searches for "STEPS fitness נתניה" or "סטודיו סטפס" surface a second, differently-named listing ("STEPS Pilates Studio," floor 3, no clear street address, with **two different wrong phone numbers** across zips.co.il and Yandex). For someone Googling without "פולג" in the query, this is genuinely confusing, and it means:
- Any citation-building work for Poleg needs to explicitly include "פולג" / "Poleg Mall" in the name or address field everywhere, so directories and Google don't conflate the two branches under one profile.
- The Naimi Mall branch's own listings are inconsistent and worth a separate cleanup pass if that branch is still active — but that's outside this site's scope.
